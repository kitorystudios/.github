## What we do

We take a product from the first sketch to the thing your users open every day — interface, API, database, deployment, and the unglamorous parts in between.

**Frontend** — Web apps and marketing sites, mobile apps, design systems, rebuilds of interfaces that have outgrown themselves.

**Backend** — APIs, authentication, payments, integrations, databases, background jobs, hosting and infrastructure.

**Whatever the project actually needs** — a one-off script, a stubborn bug someone else left behind, a migration off a platform you've outgrown, or a second pair of hands on an existing team.

## How we work

- Direct contact with the people writing the code. No account managers.
- Small, scoped pieces of work with a clear price before anything starts.
- You own the repository, the accounts and the infrastructure from day one.
- Honest estimates, including the ones you don't want to hear.
